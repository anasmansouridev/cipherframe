FAQ

"We'll just use AI ourselves."

Try it. Then count the hours your team spent, and check whether it matches the rest of your brand. You're not buying a video. You're buying 48-hour reliability and one consistent motion system, every week.

"We already have a designer."

Keep them. They're building the launch. We take the announcement backlog off their desk and hand back reusable templates.

"Why not a cheap freelancer?"

Same starting price, different promise. We deliver in 48 hours or it's free, refund you if you don't love the look, and keep every post in one brand system. Ask a freelancer for that, then compare.

How fast is it, really?

48 hours per announcement, with a style frame within 12 hours. Explainers take 8 business days; Launch Packs take 12.

What do you need from us?

Your brand files, the announcement text or link, and any partner logos. That's it.

How much does it cost?

From $200 for a single clip and $350 for a feature video. The Unlimited Desk is $1,490/month for every announcement you post. Want to test us first? Order a $200 clip; if you start a Desk within 14 days, the $200 counts toward your first month.

How do we pay?

USDC or USDT on a chain we agree in advance, or by card. Desks are paid monthly in advance. We never accept project tokens.

Is this safe?

We never send files to run, never ask you to connect a wallet, and never DM you first on Telegram. Our only official handles are listed at the bottom of this page.

Can you include our disclaimers?

Yes, exactly as you supply them. You're responsible for compliance in your markets, and we never write price or return claims.

What if we don't love it?

You approve a style frame within 12 hours. If you don't approve the first one, your first month (or the clip) is refunded.

Who owns the videos?

On full payment, we assign you all rights we hold in the final videos. Music and voice licences are passed through for your use.

Can you work on unannounced launches?

Yes. We work under NDA and keep everything private until you post.

Do you work with memecoins?

No. We work with funded or live projects in DeFi, infrastructure, wallets and payments.
